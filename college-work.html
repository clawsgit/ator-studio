Game Design @ DePaul University

Horror Level designed in Unreal Engine 5

A short Horror / Survival level that primarily focused on ambience and functionality, with a small and clear objective. I'm not a fan of building/modeling on Unreal Engine, so that was probably the biggest challenge for me. Everything else was a small scope, so the development was straight forward. This level used free assets where I could, and blocked out layouts that were not possible to fully finish in the time of the course.
